Design Patterns with Java Interview Questions

1. What is a design pattern, and how do you decide whether using one improves a design?
2. What are the creational, structural, behavioral, enterprise, and distributed pattern categories?
3. What problem does the Factory Method solve, and how would you implement it in Java?
4. What is the difference between Factory Method and Abstract Factory?
5. When would you use the Builder pattern, and how can a builder enforce object validity?
6. What are the benefits and risks of the Singleton pattern in Java?
7. How does an enum-based Singleton differ from a Spring singleton bean?
8. What problem does the Adapter pattern solve when integrating a third-party API?
9. What is the difference between Adapter, Facade, and Proxy?
10. How does the Decorator pattern add behavior without modifying the wrapped class?
11. How would you use Decorator for caching, logging, metrics, or authorization?
12. What is the Strategy pattern, and how can it replace a large conditional statement?
13. What is the Observer pattern, and when should you use a durable message broker instead?
14. How does the Command pattern help with queuing, retries, auditing, or undo operations?
15. What is the State pattern, and when is an enum state machine simpler?
16. What is the Template Method pattern, and when would composition be better than inheritance?
17. How do Repository, Service Layer, DTO, and Mapper patterns fit into a Spring Boot application?
18. What is the Specification pattern, and how can specifications be composed in Java?
19. Explain Circuit Breaker, Retry, Saga, and Transactional Outbox as distributed-system patterns.
20. What are common pattern-related anti-patterns, such as god objects, deep inheritance, and pattern overuse?
