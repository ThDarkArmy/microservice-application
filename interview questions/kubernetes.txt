Kubernetes Interview Questions

1. What is Kubernetes, and what problems does it solve beyond Docker?
2. Explain the Kubernetes control plane, worker nodes, API server, scheduler, controllers, and etcd.
3. What is a Pod, and why is it the smallest deployable unit in Kubernetes?
4. What are Deployments, ReplicaSets, Services, Namespaces, ConfigMaps, and Secrets?
5. How does Kubernetes maintain the desired state declared in a manifest?
6. What is the difference between a Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, Job, and CronJob?
7. How does a Kubernetes Service discover and route traffic to matching Pods?
8. Compare ClusterIP, NodePort, and LoadBalancer Services.
9. What is Ingress, what does an Ingress controller do, and how does it route HTTP traffic?
10. What are readiness, liveness, and startup probes, and when should each be used?
11. What are resource requests and limits, and how do they affect scheduling and reliability?
12. How do Kubernetes ConfigMaps and Secrets provide application configuration, and what are their security limitations?
13. How does Kubernetes service discovery and DNS work between namespaces?
14. How would you perform a rolling update, check its progress, and roll it back?
15. How does Horizontal Pod Autoscaling work, and why are metrics and resource requests important?
16. How do PersistentVolumes and PersistentVolumeClaims provide storage to workloads?
17. How would you secure a cluster using RBAC, ServiceAccounts, NetworkPolicies, and non-root containers?
18. How would you troubleshoot Pods in Pending, ImagePullBackOff, CrashLoopBackOff, Running-but-not-ready, or OOMKilled states?
19. How would you deploy a Java Spring Boot microservice with a Deployment, Service, ConfigMap, Secret, and health probes?
20. How would you design observability, backups, CI/CD, and disaster recovery for a production Kubernetes platform?
